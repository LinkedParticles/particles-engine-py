Brand · Voice & copy
Taglines in use, tone rules, vocabulary that must not drift

Lead line (site hero)

A git-like ledger for what an AI system believes.

Supporting lines (pick one per surface, never stack them)

  • Particles is shared memory for humans and AI agents. — README
  • AI memory you can audit and trust. — agent-memory wedge, launch demo
  • Version control for beliefs. — feature card
  • When your agent is wrong, you can see exactly why, and fix it at the source. — proof line
Plain, precise, a little dry

Sentences carry a claim, not a mood. Prefer “nothing is overwritten” to “immutable-by-design™”. Humour is allowed exactly where the docs already have it (“the honest tradeoff”, “no free lunch”).

Say the tradeoff

The product’s credibility is that it admits cost. Every “why” section pairs a benefit with what it costs (extraction runs an LLM; contradictions surface, they don’t vanish).

Vocabulary is fixed

particle, subject, corpus / deposit, extract, query, lint, review, provenance, confidence vs effective confidence, contested (a badge, never a status), supersede / retract, as-of. Statuses are UPPER_SNAKE and never paraphrased.

Three ideas carry the design

Nothing is overwritten

Beliefs are superseded or retracted, never edited. Ask what the store believed at any instant.

Trust is a read-time lens

Stored confidence is immutable; source and extractor trust are applied when you query, so a policy change re-ranks the past without rewriting it.

Contradictions are first-class

Conflicting claims surface as INCONSISTENCY particles and contested badges — visible, reviewable, and resolvable into a trust policy.
