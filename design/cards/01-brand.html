Particles Particles

The ring is a Subject; the three orbiting dots are particles — blue for a retrieval hit, green for ACTIVE, amber for contested. The core dot takes currentColor so the mark inverts with the theme. Minimum size 16 px. Clear space = one dot radius. Never recolour the orbit dots — they are the semantic palette.

Names: the product and standard are Particles; LinkedParticles is the legal / distribution identity (org, PyPI dists, linkedparticles.org). Never “Particls”, “LinkedParticles SDK”, or “Particles.ai”.
